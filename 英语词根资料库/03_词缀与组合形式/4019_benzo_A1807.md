### 1. benzodiazepine

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˌbɛnzoʊdaɪˈæzɪpiːn/

**中文释义**：n. \[化\]苯(并)二氮■( 用于制造各种镇静药)

**英文义项**：Any of a class of psychoactive drugs, structured upon diazepine, used in the treatment of anxiety, insomnia and other related disorders.

**构词拆分**：benzo- + diazepine

**词源与演化**：

From benzo- + diazepine.
