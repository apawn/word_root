## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. endothelium

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/.endəuˈθiːliəm/

**中文释义**：n. 内皮, 内种皮；\[医\] 内皮

**英文义项**：A thin layer of flat epithelial cells that lines the heart, serous cavities, lymph vessels, and blood vessels.

**构词拆分**：endo- + -thelium

**词源与演化**：

From endo- + -thelium, as in epithelium.
