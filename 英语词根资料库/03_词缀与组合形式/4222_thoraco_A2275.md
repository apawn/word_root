## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. thoracotomy

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/,θɔːrəˈkɔtəmi/

**中文释义**：\[医\] 胸廓切开术

**英文义项**：The surgical procedure of making an incision into the chest, normally as a first step to gain access to the thoracic organs, such as the heart, the lungs, and the esophagus.

**构词拆分**：thoraco- + -tomy

**词源与演化**：

From thoraco- + -tomy.
