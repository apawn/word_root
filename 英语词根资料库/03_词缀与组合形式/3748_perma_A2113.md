### 1. permafrost

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈpɜːməˌfɹɔst/；美 /ˈpɝməˌfɹɔst/

**中文释义**：n. \[地\]永久冻结带

**英文义项**：Permanently frozen ground, or a specific layer thereof.

**构词拆分**：perma- + frost

**词源与演化**：

From perma- + frost.

**例句**：

> The melting permafrost was key factor in the ecological disaster.
