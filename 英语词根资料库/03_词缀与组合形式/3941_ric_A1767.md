- 核心含义：A termination denoting jurisdiction, or a district over which government is exercised.

- 词形：-ric

**来源记录 1**（后缀／后置成分；A termination denoting jurisdiction, or a district over which government is exercised.）

From Northern Middle English -rike (Southern -riche), from Old English -rīċe (“-ric”), from rīċe (“realm, domain, jurisdiction”), from Proto-Germanic \*rīkiją (“realm, power”). Cognate with Dutch -rijk, German -reich.

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. bishopric

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbɪʃəpɹɪk/

**中文释义**：n. 主教的职位, 主教的辖区

**英文义项**：A diocese: a region in which a bishop of a church governs. / The office or function of a bishop.

**构词拆分**：bishop + -ric

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English bischopriche, from Old English bisċoprīċe, equivalent to bishop + -ric (compare German Reich).

**例句**：

> Later that year, he was appointed to the bishopric of Lindesmeere.

该用例对应义项：A diocese: a region in which a bishop of a church governs.
