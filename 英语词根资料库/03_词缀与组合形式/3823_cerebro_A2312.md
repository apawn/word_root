## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. cerebrospinal

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/.seribrəuˈspainəl/

**中文释义**：a. 脑脊髓的；\[医\] 脑脊髓的

**英文义项**：Of or relating to brain or spine (particularly the spinal cord).

**构词拆分**：From cerebro- + spinal.

**词源与演化**：

From cerebro- + spinal.

**例句**：

> There was an infection of the cerebrospinal fluid.

该用例对应义项：Of or relating to brain or spine (particularly the spinal cord).

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. cerebrovascular

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/,seribrəjˈvæskjjlə(r)/

**中文释义**：\[医\] 脑血管的

**英文义项**：Relating to the blood vessels that run to or from the brain.

**构词拆分**：From cerebro- + vascular.

**词源与演化**：

From cerebro- + vascular.
