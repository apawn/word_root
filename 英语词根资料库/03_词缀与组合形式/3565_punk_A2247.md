## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. steampunk

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈstiːm.pʌŋk/

**英文义项**：A subgenre of science fiction that depicts advanced technology combined with Victorian style and aesthetics, such as steam-powered machines and vehicles, visible gears and screws and people dressed in 19th-century attires. / A writer of steampunk fiction.

**构词拆分**：steam + -punk

**词源与演化**：

From steam + -punk, by analogy with cyberpunk, coined by science-fiction writer Kevin Wayne Jeter (born 1950) in a 1987 letter to the magazine Locus in response to a review of his book Infernal Devices published the same year (see the quotation below).

**例句**：

> One can consider Esperanto as a Steampunk language, whilst Interlingua as a Dieselpunk language.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. cyberpunk

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈsaɪ.bə.pʌŋk/；美 /ˈsaɪ.bɚˌpʌŋk/

**中文释义**：n. 计算机科幻小说； 网络黑客

**英文义项**：A subgenre of science fiction which focuses on computer or information technology and virtual reality juxtaposed with a degree of breakdown or radical change in the social order. / A cyberpunk character, a hacker punk, a high-tech low life.

**构词拆分**：cyber- + -punk

**词源与演化**：

From cyber- + -punk, coined by American writer and software developer Bruce Bethke as the title of a 1983 short story, and later popularized by Gardner Dozois.

**例句**：

> The film The Matrix redefined what a cyberpunk looked like.

该用例对应义项：A cyberpunk character, a hacker punk, a high-tech low life.
