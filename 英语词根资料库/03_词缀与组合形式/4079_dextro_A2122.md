### 1. dextrose

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈdɛkstɹəʊz/

**中文释义**：n. 葡萄糖, 右旋糖；\[化\] 葡萄糖; 葡糖

**英文义项**：The naturally occurring dextrorotatory form of glucose monosaccharide molecule.

**构词拆分**：dextro- + -ose

**词源与演化**：

From dextro- + -ose (“right-handed sugar”).
