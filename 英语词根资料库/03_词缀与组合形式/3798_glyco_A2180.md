### 2. glycolysis

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ɡlaɪˈkɒlɪsɪs/

**中文释义**：n. 糖酵解(作用)；\[化\] 糖酵解

**英文义项**：The cellular degradation of the simple sugar glucose to yield pyruvic acid (pyruvate), and ATP as an energy source

**构词拆分**：glyco- + -lysis

**词源与演化**：

From glyco- + -lysis.
