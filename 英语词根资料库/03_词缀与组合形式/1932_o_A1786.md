# 1932 · -o — suf. 用以构成名词或形容词,表示具有…性质(的), 与…有关的; 用以构成感叹词

[总目录](../00_总目录_按常用程度.md)

**类型**：词缀／组合形式　｜　**来源语言**：见词源记录

## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：suf. 用以构成名词或形容词,表示具有…性质(的), 与…有关的; 用以构成感叹词
- 英文含义：A colloquializing suffix, typically appended to names, abbreviations of long words, or substantive uses of adjectives.
- 词形：-o

**多个词源／构词路径**：同一拼写可能有不同来源；以下按词性和含义分别列出。

**来源记录 1**（后缀／后置成分；A colloquializing suffix, typically appended to names, abbreviations of long words, or substantive uses of adjectives.）

Originally a special use of the interjection O or oh appended to street cries and nautical calls to project a hail or shout (as in milk-oh, smoke-oh, and sail-ho). Its later use as a colloquializing noun suffix, particularly prominent in Australian English, developed from the substantivation of these cries (e.g., smoko), reinforced by early nautical adaptations of Mediterranean Lingua Franca or pseudo‐Spanish words (such as guardo). As noted by the Australian National Dictionary, this vocative “hailing” origin naturally led to its frequent attachment to personal names to form nicknames (e.g., John‐o, Jacko). Some senses may also derive from o (“one”), from Middle English o, oo, a variant of a, on, oon, an (“one”). See one and -y.

**来源记录 2**（后缀／后置成分；Converts certain words to faux Italian or Spanish. Can be used with Spanish el for expressions such as el stinko.）

From many Spanish or Italian words that end in o. This ending in such Spanish or Italian words generally derives from -um, the accusative singular inflectional ending for masculine and neuter nouns in Latin.

**来源记录 3**（后缀／后置成分；Added to verb stems to create a noun describing an error relating to the action described by the verb.）

Rebracketing of typo.

## 逐词详解

### 1. combo

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈkɒmbəʊ/

**中文释义**：n. 小型爵士乐团

**英文义项**：A small musical group. / A combination.

**构词拆分**：combination + -o

**词源与演化**：

Clipping of combination + -o.

**例句**：

> I order the low priced combo platter: a taco, a burrito and a chimichanga.

该用例对应义项：A combination.

### 2. psycho

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈsaɪ̯.kəʊ̯/；美 /ˈsaɪ̯.koʊ̯/

**中文释义**：n. 心理分析, 精神神经病患者；a. 精神变态的, 精神神经病的

**英文义项**：A person who is psychotic, psychopathic, or (broadly) otherwise insane. / A person who acts in a bizarre or dangerous manner.

**词源与演化**：

Clipping of psychotic or psychopath + -o (“person with characteristic”). Compare sicko.

该用例对应义项：A person who acts in a bizarre or dangerous manner.
