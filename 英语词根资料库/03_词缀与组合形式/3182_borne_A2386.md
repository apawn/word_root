## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. airborne

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈєəbɒːn/

**中文释义**：a. 升空的, 空运的, 空中传播的, 空降的

**英文义项**：In or carried by the air. / In flight.

**构词拆分**：air + -borne

**词源与演化**：

Attested since the 1640s; air + -borne

**例句**：

> Airborne pollen can aggravate allergies.

该用例对应义项：In or carried by the air.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. waterborne

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈwɒtəbɒːn/

**中文释义**：a. 浮起的, 水传播的, 水运的

**英文义项**：Transported or transmitted by water. / Floating on the water; afloat.

**构词拆分**：water + -borne

**词源与演化**：

From water + -borne.

**例句**：

> Without water, the risk of waterborne diseases increases in the population.
