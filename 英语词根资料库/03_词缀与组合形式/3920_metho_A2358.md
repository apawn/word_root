## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：表示“甲基”之义(元音前作meth-)
- 英文含义：Methyl.
- 词形：metho-

**来源记录 1**（前缀／前置成分；Methyl.）

From methyl + -o-. Ultimately from Ancient Greek μέθυ (méthu, “wine”).

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. methotrexate

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˌmɛθ.əˈtɹɛk.seɪt/；美 /ˌmɛθ.əˈtɹɛkˌseɪt/

**中文释义**：\[化\] 甲氨蝶呤; 氨甲蝶呤

**英文义项**：An antimetabolite and antifolate drug used in treatment of cancer, autoimmune diseases, and ectopic pregnancies.

**构词拆分**：metho- + tre- (“alteration of pteridine”) + (carbo)x(yl)ate

**词源与演化**：

Probably from metho- + tre- (“alteration of pteridine”) + (carbo)x(yl)ate.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. omeprazole

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /əʊˈmɛp.ɹə.zəʊl/；美 /oʊˈmɛp.ɹəˌzoʊl/

**中文释义**：\[医\]奥美拉唑洛赛克, H2受体拮抗剂, 治疗消化道溃疡。

**英文义项**：A substituted benzimidazole (trademark Losec, Prilosec) which is a proton pump inhibitor and inhibits secretion of gastric acid, being given orally in the treatment of ulcers and gastroesophageal reflux; 5-methoxy-2-(4-methoxy-3,5-dimethyl-2-pyridylmethylsulfinyl)benzimidazole, C₁₇H₁₉N₃O₃S.

**构词拆分**：oxy- + metho- + -prazole

**词源与演化**：

From o(xy)- + me(tho)- + -prazole (“benzimidazole derivative”).
