### 1. histidine

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈhɪstɪdiːn/

**中文释义**：n. \[生化\]组氨酸, 组织氨基酸

**英文义项**：An essential amino acid C₆H₉N₃O₂ found in most animal proteins; essential for tissue growth and repair.

**构词拆分**：histo- + -idine

**词源与演化**：

From histo- + -idine.
