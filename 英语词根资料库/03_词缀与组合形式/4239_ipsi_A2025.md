## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. ipsilateral

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˌɪpsɪˈlætəɹəl/

**中文释义**：\[医\] 同侧的

**英文义项**：On the same side of the body.

**构词拆分**：From ipsi- + lateral.

**词源与演化**：

From ipsi- + lateral.

**例句**：

> The stroke was characterised by ipsilateral paralysis.

该用例对应义项：On the same side of the body.
