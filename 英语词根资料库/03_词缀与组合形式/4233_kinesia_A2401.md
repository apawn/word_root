## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. dyskinesia

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：美 /dɪs.kəˈniʒə/

**中文释义**：n. 运动障碍, 动作困难；\[医\] 运动障碍

**英文义项**：A state of involuntary muscle movements, like tics and torticollis, and reduced voluntary muscle movements. / A disorder involving such state.

**构词拆分**：dys- + -kinesia

**词源与演化**：

From dys- + -kinesia.
