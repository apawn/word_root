### 1. retinoic

**词性**：形容词

**中文释义**：\[医\]Tretinoin

**英文义项**：Derived from retinol; relating to retinoic acid or its derivatives

**构词拆分**：From retina + -oic.

**词源与演化**：

From retina + -oic.

**例句**：

> Tom's liver quickly turned the sardines into retinoic acid.
