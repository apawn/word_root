## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. mesoderm

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈmesəudəːm/

**中文释义**：n. 中胚层；\[医\] 中胚层

**英文义项**：One of the three tissue layers in the embryo of a metazoan animal, which will produce, through development, many internal organs of the adult, e.g. muscles, spine and the circulatory system.

**构词拆分**：meso- + -derm

**词源与演化**：

From meso- + -derm.
