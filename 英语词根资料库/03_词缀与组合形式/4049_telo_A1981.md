## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. telomere

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈtɛləmɪəɹ/

**中文释义**：\[医\] 端粒(染色体)

**英文义项**：Either of the sequences of DNA at each end of a eukaryotic chromosome.

**构词拆分**：telo- (“end”) + -mere (“part”)

**词源与演化**：

From telo- (“end”) + -mere (“part”).
