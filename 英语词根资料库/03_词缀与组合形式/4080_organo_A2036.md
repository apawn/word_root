### 1. organometallic

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ɔː,gænəʊmɪˈtælɪk/

**中文释义**：a. 有机金属的

**英文义项**：of, or relating to organic compounds having a metal atom directly bonded to a carbon atom

**构词拆分**：From organo- + metallic.
