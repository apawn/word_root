- 核心含义：place
- 英文含义：place

**来源记录 1**（后缀／后置成分；place）

From Latin topica, from Ancient Greek τοπικός (topikós, “pertaining to a place, local, pertaining to a common place, or topic, topical”), from τόπος (tópos, “a place”); equivalent to -topia + -ic.
