### 1. zooplankton

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈzəʊ.ə.plaŋ(k).tən/；美 /ˌzoʊ.əˈplæŋ(k).tən/

**中文释义**：\[医\] 浮游动物

**英文义项**：Free-floating small protozoa, crustaceans (such as krill), etc. and the eggs and larvae from larger animals.

**构词拆分**：zoo- + plankton

**词源与演化**：

From zoo- + plankton.
