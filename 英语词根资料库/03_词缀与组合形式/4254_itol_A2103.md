## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. xylitol

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈzaɪlɪtɒl/；美 /ˈzaɪlɪtɑl/

**中文释义**：n. \[化\]木糖醇

**英文义项**：A pentahydric alcohol, C₅H₁₂O₅, penta-hydroxy pentane; derived from xylose; used as a sweetener.

**词源与演化**：

From Ancient Greek ξύλον (xúlon, “wood”) + English -itol (“anhydric alcohol”).
