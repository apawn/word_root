## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. velveteen

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈvɛlvətiːn/

**中文释义**：n. 棉绒, 平绒

**英文义项**：A cotton fabric with a short pile, resembling velvet.

**构词拆分**：velvet + -een

**词源与演化**：

From velvet + -een. Compare veloutine, from French.

**例句**：

> It was a boy dressed in ragged velveteen breeches, and thin discoloured shirt.
