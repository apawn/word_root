## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. plagioclase

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈpleɪ.d͡ʒɪ.əˌkleɪz/；美 /ˈpleɪ.d͡ʒi.əˌkleɪs/

**中文释义**：n. 斜长岩；\[化\] 斜长石

**英文义项**：Any of a group of aluminum silicate feldspathic minerals ranging in their ratio of calcium to sodium.

**构词拆分**：plagio- + -clase

**词源与演化**：

From plagio- + -clase, after German Plagioklas.
