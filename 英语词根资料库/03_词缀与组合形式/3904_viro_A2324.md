## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. virology

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/vaɪˈɹɒləd͡ʒi/

**中文释义**：n. 病毒学；\[医\] 病毒学

**英文义项**：The branch of microbiology that deals with the study of viruses and viral diseases.

**构词拆分**：viro- + -logy

**词源与演化**：

From viro- + -logy.

**例句**：

> The Wuhan virology institute was only studying viruses that came directly from bats.
