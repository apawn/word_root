## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. nephrology

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/nəˈfɹɒlədʒi/

**中文释义**：n. 肾脏学

**英文义项**：The branch of medicine that deals with the function and diseases of the kidneys.

**构词拆分**：nephro- + -logy

**词源与演化**：

From nephro- + -logy.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. nephropathy

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/niˈfrɔpəθi/

**中文释义**：\[医\] 肾病

**英文义项**：Damage to, disease of, or abnormality of the kidneys.

**构词拆分**：nephro- + -pathy

**词源与演化**：

From nephro- + -pathy.
