## 逐词详解

### 1. lactase

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈlækteis/

**中文释义**：n. 乳糖酶；\[化\] 乳糖酶

**英文义项**：A β-galactosidase enzyme that is involved in the hydrolysis of the disaccharide lactose into constituent galactose and glucose monomers.

**构词拆分**：lacto- + -ase

**词源与演化**：

From lacto- + -ase.
