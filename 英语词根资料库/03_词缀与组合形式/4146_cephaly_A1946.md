## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. microcephaly

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：美 /ˌmaɪkɹoʊˈsɛfəli/

**中文释义**：n. 小头畸型；\[医\] 小头

**英文义项**：A neurological disorder in which the person affected has an abnormally small head due to a failure of brain growth.

**构词拆分**：micro- + -cephaly

**词源与演化**：

From micro- + -cephaly.
