## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：\[医\]〔后缀〕意为与呼吸有关, 如端坐呼吸(orthopnea)
- 英文含义：breathing, respiration
- 词形：-pnea

**来源记录 1**（后缀／后置成分；breathing, respiration）

Representing -πνοια (-pnoia), the common terminal element of the Ancient Greek ἄπνοια (ápnoia, “apnea”), δύσπνοια (dúspnoia, “dyspnea”), εὔπνοια (eúpnoia, “eupnea”), ὀρθόπνοια (orthópnoia, “orthopnea”), etc, from πνέω (pnéō, “I blow”) and πνοή (pnoḗ, “breath, breathing”).

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. apnea

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈæp.ni.ə/

**中文释义**：n. 呼吸暂停, 窒息；\[医\] 呼吸暂停, 窒息

**英文义项**：The cessation of breathing, most often in reference to transient instances thereof during sleep.

**构词拆分**：a- + -pnea

**词源与演化**：

From a- + -pnea, from the New Latin apnoea, from the Ancient Greek ἄπνοια (ápnoia), formed from ἀ- (a-, “absence of”) + πνοή (pnoḗ, “breathing”).

**例句**：

> Sleep apnea occurs most often when sleeping in a supine position.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. dyspnea

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /dɪspˈniː.ə/；美 /ˈdɪsp.ni.ə/

**中文释义**：n. 呼吸困难；\[医\] 呼吸困难

**英文义项**：Difficult or laboured breathing.

**构词拆分**：dys- (prefix meaning ‘abnormal; difficult; disease’) + -pnea (suffix meaning ‘breathing, respiration’)

**词源与演化**：

Variant spelling of dyspnoea, a learned borrowing from Latin dyspnoea (“difficulty breathing”), from Ancient Greek δῠ́σπνοιᾰ (dŭ́spnoiă, “difficulty breathing, shortness of breath”), from δῠ́σπνοος (dŭ́spnoos, “short of breath, breathing with difficulty”, adjective) (also δῠ́σπνους (dŭ́spnous) by contraction) + -ῐᾰ (-ĭă, suffix forming abstract nouns). Δῠ́σπνοος (Dŭ́spnoos) is derived from δῠσ- (dŭs-, prefix meaning ‘bad; difficult, hard; unfortunate’) + πνέω (pnéō, “to blow; to breathe”) (from Proto-Indo-European \*pnew- (“to breathe; to pant”)) + -ος (-os, suffix forming adjectives). The English word is analysable as dys- (prefix meaning ‘abnormal; difficult; disease’) + -pnea (suffix meaning ‘breathing, respiration’).

**例句**：

> Dyspnea is when a person has shortness of breath.
