## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. biopsy

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：美 /ˈbaɪɑpsi/

**中文释义**：n. 活组织检查；\[医\] 活组织检查

**英文义项**：The removal and examination of a sample of tissue, cells, or bodily fluid from a living body for diagnostic purposes.

**构词拆分**：bi- + -opsy

**词源与演化**：

From French biopsie, from bi- + -opsy.

**例句**：

> We will need to perform a biopsy to determine whether the tumour is malignant or benign.

该用例对应义项：The removal and examination of a sample of tissue, cells, or bodily fluid from a living body for diagnostic purposes.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. necropsy

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈnɛkɹɒpsi/；美 /ˈnɛkɹɑpsi/

**中文释义**：n. 验尸；\[医\] 尸体剖检

**英文义项**：The pathological examination of a corpse, particularly to determine cause of death.

**构词拆分**：necro- + -opsy

**词源与演化**：

From necro- + -opsy.
