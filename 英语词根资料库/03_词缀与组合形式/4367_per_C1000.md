## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. peridium

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/pəˈridiəm/

**中文释义**：n. \[植\]( 某些真菌的) 包被

**英文义项**：The outer layer that covers the spore-bearing organ in many fungi.

**词源与演化**：

A New Latin form of Ancient Greek πηρίδιον (pērídion, “little pouch”).
