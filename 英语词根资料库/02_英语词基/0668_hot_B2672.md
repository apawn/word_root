## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. hot

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /hɒt/；美 /hɑt/

**中文释义**：a. 热的, 热心的, 辣的, 热情的, 激动的, 猛烈的, 紧迫的；adv. 热, 紧迫地

**英文义项**：Having or giving off a high temperature. / Feeling the sensation of heat, especially to the point of discomfort.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English hot, hoot, from Old English hāt (“hot”), from Proto-West Germanic \*hait, from Proto-Germanic \*haitaz (“hot”), from Proto-Indo-European \*keHy- (“to heat; to become hot”). Cognates Cognate with Yola hoat, hoate, hote (“hot”), West Frisian hjit, hyt (“hot”), Bavarian hoaß (“hot”), Central Franconian heeß (“hot”), Dutch heet (“hot”), German heiss, heiß, heyß (“hot”), Vilamovian haas, haes, hȧś, hǡs (“hot”), Yiddish הייס (heys, “hot”), Danish hed (“hot”), Faroese and Icelandic heitur (“hot”), Norwegian Bokmål and Swedish het (“hot”); also Lithuanian kaitra (“heat”). Related to heat. Superseded non-native Middle English chaud, from Old French chaut (“hot”); and early Modern English calent, from Latin calēns (“hot”).

**例句**：

> I wouldn't speed through here if I was you. This area is hot this time of night.

该用例对应义项：Characterized by police presence or activity.

### 2. hotline

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈhɔtlain/

**中文释义**：n. 热线电话, 咨询电话；热线

**英文义项**：A telephone line that is always staffed and able to give immediate assistance. / A direct line between two people, especially one between heads of state to be used in an emergency.

**构词拆分**：hot + line

**词源与演化**：

From hot + line.

**例句**：

> If you've got any problems with the product, ring our hotline.

该用例对应义项：A telephone line that is always staffed and able to give immediate assistance.

### 4. hotly

**词性**：副词　｜　**音标**：/ˈhɒtli/

**中文释义**：adv. 暑热地, 激烈地, 热心地

**英文义项**：With great amounts of heat. / In a heated manner; intensely or vehemently.

**构词拆分**：hot + -ly
