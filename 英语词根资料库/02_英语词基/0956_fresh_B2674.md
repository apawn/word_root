- 核心含义：a. 新鲜的, 新奇的, 另外的, 淡的, 精神饱满的, 冒失的；adv. 最新地, 刚刚；n. 开始, 泛滥

- 词形：fresh

**多个词源／构词路径**：同一拼写可能有不同来源；以下按词性和含义分别列出。

**来源记录 1**（形容词；Newly produced or obtained; recent.）

From Middle English fressh, from Old English fersċ (“fresh, pure, sweet”), from Proto-West Germanic \*frisk (“fresh”), from Proto-Germanic \*friskaz (“fresh”), from Proto-Indo-European \*preysk- (“fresh”). The verb is from Middle English freshen (“to freshen”), from the adjective. Cognate with Scots fresch (“fresh”), West Frisian farsk (“fresh”), Dutch vers (“fresh”), Walloon frexh (“fresh”), German frisch (“fresh”), French frais (“fresh”), Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, and Norwegian Nynorsk frisk (“fresh”), Swedish frisk (“well, fresh”), Icelandic ferskur (“fresh”), Lithuanian prėskas (“unflavoured, tasteless, fresh”), Russian пре́сный (présnyj, “sweet, fresh, unleavened, tasteless”). Doublet of fresco and frisk. Slang sense possibly shortened form of “fresh out the pack”, 1980s routine by Grand Wizzard Theodore.

**来源记录 2**（形容词；Rude, cheeky, or inappropriate; presumptuous; disrespectful; forward.）

First use appears c. 1848, US slang, probably from German frech (“impudent, cheeky, insolent”), from Middle High German vrech (“bold, brave, lively”), from Old High German freh (“greedy, eager, avaricious, covetous”), from Proto-West Germanic \*frek, from Proto-Germanic \*frekaz (“greedy, outrageous, courageous, capable, active”), from Proto-Indo-European \*preg- (“to be quick, twitch, sprinkle, splash”). Cognate with Old English frec (“greedy; eager, bold, daring; dangerous”) and Danish fræk (“naughty”). More at freak.

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. fresh

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/fɹɛʃ/

**中文释义**：a. 新鲜的, 新奇的, 另外的, 淡的, 精神饱满的, 冒失的；adv. 最新地, 刚刚；n. 开始, 泛滥

**英文义项**：Newly produced or obtained; recent. / Not dried, frozen, or spoiled.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English fressh, from Old English fersċ (“fresh, pure, sweet”), from Proto-West Germanic \*frisk (“fresh”), from Proto-Germanic \*friskaz (“fresh”), from Proto-Indo-European \*preysk- (“fresh”). The verb is from Middle English freshen (“to freshen”), from the adjective. Cognate with Scots fresch (“fresh”), West Frisian farsk (“fresh”), Dutch vers (“fresh”), Walloon frexh (“fresh”), German frisch (“fresh”), French frais (“fresh”), Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, and Norwegian Nynorsk frisk (“fresh”), Swedish frisk (“well, fresh”), Icelandic ferskur (“fresh”), Lithuanian prėskas (“unflavoured, tasteless, fresh”), Russian пре́сный (présnyj, “sweet, fresh, unleavened, tasteless”). Doublet of fresco and frisk. Slang sense possibly shortened form of “fresh out the pack”, 1980s routine by Grand Wizzard Theodore.

**例句**：

> With his recent divorce still fresh in his mind, he was unable to concentrate on his work.

该用例对应义项：Newly produced or obtained; recent.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. freshman

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹɛʃ.mən/

**中文释义**：n. 新手, 生手, 大学一年级学生, (美)中学一年级学生

**英文义项**：a person (of either sex) entering the first year of an institution, especially a high school (ninth grade for US, grade 9 for Canada, grade 7 for Philippines), a university, or legislative body

**构词拆分**：fresh + man

**词源与演化**：

From fresh + man.

**例句**：

> At the time I was a wide-eyed freshman, but I was soon to grow jaded and cynical.

该用例对应义项：a person (of either sex) entering the first year of an institution, especially a high school (ninth grade for US, grade 9 for Canada, grade 7 for Philippines), a university, or legislative body

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. refresh

**词性**：动词　｜　**音标**：英 /ɹiˈfɹɛʃ/；美 /ɹɪˈfɹɛʃ/

**中文释义**：vt. 使清新, 使恢复, 使生气蓬勃；vi. 提起精神, 恢复精神, 吃点心, 喝饮料；vt. 刷新, 重新整理

**英文义项**：To renew or revitalize. / To become fresh again; to be revitalized.

**构词拆分**：re- + fresh

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English refreshen, refreschen, refrisschen, from Old French refrescher (“to refresh”) (modern French rafraîchir), equivalent to re- + fresh.

**例句**：

> Sleep refreshes the body and the mind.

该用例对应义项：To renew or revitalize.

<a id="w-4"></a>

### 4. freshly

**词性**：副词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹɛʃli/

**中文释义**：adv. 新, 新近, 精神饱满

**英文义项**：Recently, newly. / In a rude or impertinent manner.

**构词拆分**：fresh + -ly

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English freschely, freschliche (also as ferscheli, fersly, ferselich), equivalent to fresh + -ly.

**例句**：

> The freshly picked flowers will wilt in a few days but for now are still fragrant.

该用例对应义项：Recently, newly.

<a id="w-5"></a>

### 5. freshwater

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈfɹɛʃˌwɔːtə(ɹ)/；/ˈfɹɛʃˌwɔtər/

**中文释义**：a. 江河湖泊的, 淡水的

**英文义项**：Living in fresh water. / Consisting of fresh water.

**构词拆分**：fresh + water

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English freche watur, fresshe water; equivalent to fresh + water.

**例句**：

> Lake Baikal is the world's largest freshwater lake in terms of volume.

该用例对应义项：Consisting of fresh water.

### 6. freshness

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹɛʃnəs/

**中文释义**：n. 新, 新鲜, 精神饱满

**英文义项**：The state or quality of being fresh. / Impudence; cheek.

**构词拆分**：fresh + -ness

**词源与演化**：

From fresh + -ness.

**用法示例／搭配（非完整句）**：

> the clear freshness of water from a mountain spring

该用例对应义项：The state or quality of being fresh.

<a id="w-7"></a>

### 7. freshen

**词性**：动词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹɛʃən/

**中文释义**：v. (使)显得新鲜, (使)精神饱满, (使)减少咸味

**英文义项**：To be refreshed. / To become cool.

**构词拆分**：fresh + -en

**词源与演化**：

From fresh + -en.

**用法示例／搭配（非完整句）**：

> to freshen water, fish, or flesh

该用例对应义项：To make less salty; to separate, as water, from saline ingredients.

<a id="w-8"></a>

### 8. afresh

**词性**：副词　｜　**音标**：美 /ʌˈfɹɛʃ/

**中文释义**：adv. 重新

**英文义项**：Anew; again; once more.

**构词拆分**：a- + fresh

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English a-fresche, equivalent to a- + fresh. Compare Old English afersċean (“to freshen; become fresh”).

**例句**：

> We ditched the original design and decided to start afresh.

该用例对应义项：Anew; again; once more.
