### 2. footstool

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfʊtˌstuːl/

**中文释义**：n. 脚凳

**英文义项**：A low stool for supporting the feet while seated. / Anything trodden upon or treated as subservient.

**构词拆分**：foot + stool

**词源与演化**：

From foot + stool.

**例句**：
