## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 类型, 流派
- 英文含义：A kind; a stylistic category or sort, especially of literature or other artworks.
- 词形：genre

**来源记录 1**（名词；A kind; a stylistic category or sort, especially of literature or other artworks.）

Unadapted borrowing from French genre, from Old French gen(d)re, borrowed from Latin genere. Doublet of gender and genus.

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. genre

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈ(d)ʒɒnɹə/；美 /ˈ(d)ʒɑnɹə/

**中文释义**：n. 类型, 流派

**英文义项**：A kind; a stylistic category or sort, especially of literature or other artworks.

**词源与演化**：

Unadapted borrowing from French genre, from Old French gen(d)re, borrowed from Latin genere. Doublet of gender and genus.

**例句**：

> The still life has been a popular genre in painting since the 17th century.

该用例对应义项：A kind; a stylistic category or sort, especially of literature or other artworks.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. subgenre

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈsʌb,dʒeŋrə/

**中文释义**：亚种；亚美

**英文义项**：One of several categories within a particular genre.

**构词拆分**：sub- + genre

**词源与演化**：

From sub- + genre.

**例句**：

> He thinks he listens to phonk music, but in reality he only listens to a subgenre of phonk, called drift phonk.
