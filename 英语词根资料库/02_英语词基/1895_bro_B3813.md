## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. bro

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbɹəʊ̯/；美 /ˈbɹoʊ̯/

**中文释义**：abbr. 兄弟（brother）

**英文义项**：Brother (a male sibling). / Brother (a comrade or friend; one who shares one’s ideals).

**例句**：

> My mom took my lil' bro to soccer practice now and she wanted me to pick him up.

该用例对应义项：Brother (a male sibling).

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. cro

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /kɹəʊ/；美 /kɹoʊ/

**中文释义**：阴极射线示波器（Cathode-Ray Oscillograph）

**英文义项**：Synonym of bro (“a male comrade or friend”).

**构词拆分**：c- + bro

**词源与演化**：

From c- (“Crip”) + bro.
