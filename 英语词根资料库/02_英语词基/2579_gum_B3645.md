**词源与演化**：

From Middle English gom, gome, gomme, goome, gum, gume, gumme, from Old English gōma (“palate”), from Proto-West Germanic \*gōmō, from Proto-Germanic \*gaumô, \*gōmô (“palate”), from Proto-Indo-European \*ǵʰeh₂- (“to gape, yawn”).
