## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. myth

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/mɪθ/

**中文释义**：n. 神话, 虚构的事, 虚构的人

**英文义项**：A traditional story which embodies a belief regarding some fact or phenomenon of experience, and in which often the forces of nature and of the soul are personified; a sacred narrative regarding a god, a hero, the origin of the world or of a people, etc. / Such stories as a genre.

**词源与演化**：

From Ancient Greek μῦθος (mûthos, “word, humour, companion, speech, account, rumour, fable”). Attested in English since 1830. Doublet of mythos.

**例句**：

> Scientists debunk the myth that gum stays in the human stomach for seven years.

该用例对应义项：A commonly-held but false belief, a common misconception; a fictitious or imaginary person or thing; a popular conception about a real person or event which exaggerates or idealizes reality.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. mythic

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈmɪθɪk/

**中文释义**：a. 神话的, 存在于神话中的, 神话式的, 不真实的, 虚构的, 幻想出来的

**英文义项**：Mythical; existing in myth. / Very rare.

**构词拆分**：myth + -ic

**词源与演化**：

1660s; from Latin mȳthicus or Ancient Greek μυθικός (muthikós); equivalent to myth + -ic.

**例句**：

> A novel of almost mythic consequence.

该用例对应义项：relating to or having the nature of myth
