## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. buzz

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/bʌz/

**中文释义**：n. 嗡嗡声, 流言；vi. 发出嗡嗡声, 说闲话；vt. 使嗡嗡叫, 散布

**英文义项**：A continuous humming noise, as of bees; a confused murmur, as of general conversation in low tones. / A whisper.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English \*bussen (suggested by Middle English bussyng (“buzzing”)), of onomatopoeic origin. Cognate with Scots bizz (“to buzz”). Compare Middle English bunning (“buzzing”), Middle English hossing (“buzzing”), Middle English bissen (“to hush”).

**例句**：

> Still feeling the buzz from the coffee, he pushed through the last of the homework.

该用例对应义项：A rush or feeling of energy or excitement; a feeling of slight intoxication.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. buzzing

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbʌzɪŋ/

**中文释义**：a. 嗡嗡响的, 营营响的；\[电\] 发蜂音

**英文义项**：The sound produced by something that buzzes.

**构词拆分**：buzz + -ing

**词源与演化**：

From buzz + -ing.

**例句**：

> I can hear buzzing coming from the television.

该用例对应义项：The sound produced by something that buzzes.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. buzzer

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbʌzə/；美 /ˈbʌzɚ/

**中文释义**：n. 嗡嗡作声的东西, 蜂音器；\[化\] 蜂鸣器; 磨轮; 砂轮

**英文义项**：One who, or that which, buzzes; an insect that buzzes. / A device that makes a buzzing sound.

**构词拆分**：buzz + -er

**词源与演化**：

From buzz + -er.

**例句**：

> If you think you know the answer to the question, hit the buzzer as fast as you can.

该用例对应义项：A device that makes a buzzing sound.

<a id="w-4"></a>

### 4. buzzword

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbʌzwɜː(ɹ)d/；美 /ˈbʌzwɜɹd/

**中文释义**：n. 流行词

**英文义项**：A word drawn from, or imitative of, technical jargon, used more to impress others than to convey meaning.

**构词拆分**：buzz + word

**词源与演化**：

Coined in the 1970s in the United States, from buzz + word.

**例句**：

> Their salespeople know all the right buzzwords, but they can't really help you solve your problems.

该用例对应义项：A word drawn from, or imitative of, technical jargon, used more to impress others than to convey meaning.

<a id="w-5"></a>

### 5. buzzy

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈbʌzi/

**中文释义**：n. 伸缩式风钻

**英文义项**：Having a buzzing sound. / Being the subject of cultural buzz.

**构词拆分**：buzz + -y

**词源与演化**：

From buzz + -y.

<a id="w-6"></a>

### 6. buzzkill

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：美 /ˈbʌzkɪl/

**中文释义**：n. 扫兴、泼冷水的人或事 (something or someone with a dispiriting effect)

**英文义项**：Something that or someone who spoils an otherwise enjoyable event. / A person who attends a social event and occupies their time with unsociable activities.

**构词拆分**：buzz (“feeling of energy or excitement”) + kill

**词源与演化**：

From buzz (“feeling of energy or excitement”) + kill.

<a id="w-7"></a>

### 7. abuzz

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /əˈbʌz/

**中文释义**：a. 嗡嗡的, 嘈杂的

**英文义项**：Characterized by a high level of activity or gossip; in a buzz (“feeling or rush of energy or excitement”), buzzing.

**构词拆分**：a- (prefix indicating a condition or manner) + buzz (“feeling or rush of energy or excitement; major topic of conversation; widespread rumor; information spread behind the scenes”)

**词源与演化**：

From a- (prefix indicating a condition or manner) + buzz (“feeling or rush of energy or excitement; major topic of conversation; widespread rumor; information spread behind the scenes”) or buzz (“to show a high level of activity and haste; to communicate in an undertone; to spread, as a report, by whispers or secretly; to talk to incessantly or confidentially in a low humming voice”).

**例句**：

> Mary's kitchen was abuzz with bubbling pots and frying cheese.
