## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. famous

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfeɪ.məs/

**中文释义**：a. 出名的, 极好的

**英文义项**：Well known. / In the public eye.

**构词拆分**：fame + -ous

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English famous, from Anglo-Norman famous, from Latin fāmōsus, equivalent to fame + -ous. Displaced native Old English hlīsful.

**例句**：

> Some people are only famous within their city.

该用例对应义项：In the public eye.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. famously

**词性**：副词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfeiməsli/

**中文释义**：adv. 极好地, 非常令人满意地

**英文义项**：In a celebrated manner. / Indicates that the act, state, or occurrence described by the sentence is famous. In such a manner as to become famous or produce something that would become famous.

**构词拆分**：famous + -ly

**词源与演化**：

From famous + -ly.

**例句**：

> The new roommates got on famously.

该用例对应义项：Really well, having great rapport.
