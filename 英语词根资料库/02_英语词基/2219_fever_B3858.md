## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 发烧, 发热, 热病；\[医\] 发热, 热
- 英文含义：A higher than normal body temperature of a person (or, generally, a mammal), usually caused by disease.
- 词形：fever

**来源记录 1**（名词；A higher than normal body temperature of a person (or, generally, a mammal), usually caused by disease.）

From Middle English fever, fevere, from Old English fefer, fefor (“fever”) and Old French fievre (“fever”), from Latin febris (“a fever”), from Proto-Indo-European \*dʰegʷʰ- (“to burn”). Displaced native Old English hriþ (“fever”). Compare also Saterland Frisian Fiewer, German Fieber, Danish feber, Swedish feber.

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. fever

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈfiːvə/；美 /ˈfivɚ/

**中文释义**：n. 发烧, 发热, 热病；\[医\] 发热, 热

**英文义项**：A higher than normal body temperature of a person (or, generally, a mammal), usually caused by disease. / Any of various diseases.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English fever, fevere, from Old English fefer, fefor (“fever”) and Old French fievre (“fever”), from Latin febris (“a fever”), from Proto-Indo-European \*dʰegʷʰ- (“to burn”). Displaced native Old English hriþ (“fever”). Compare also Saterland Frisian Fiewer, German Fieber, Danish feber, Swedish feber.

**例句**：

> There has been a rise in my fever. I think I've the flu.

该用例对应义项：A higher than normal body temperature of a person (or, generally, a mammal), usually caused by disease.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. feverish

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈfiː.və.ɹɪʃ/；美 /ˈfi.vɚ.ɪʃ/

**中文释义**：a. 发热的, 极度兴奋的, 不安的

**英文义项**：Having a fever, an elevated body temperature. / Filled with excess energy.

**构词拆分**：fever + -ish

**词源与演化**：

From fever + -ish.

**例句**：

> The illness made him feverish, so they applied cold compresses.

该用例对应义项：Having a fever, an elevated body temperature.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. fevered

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfiːvəd/

**中文释义**：a. 发烧的, 激动的, 激昂的

**英文义项**：Affected by a fever; feverish. / Heated; impassioned; enthusiastic to the point of distraction.

**构词拆分**：From fever + -ed.

**词源与演化**：

From fever + -ed.
