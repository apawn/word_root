# 0320 · news — n. 新闻, 消息, 报导；\[法\] 新闻, 消息, 新闻报导

[总目录](../00_总目录_按常用程度.md)

**类型**：英语自由词基　｜　**来源语言**：见词源记录

## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 新闻, 消息, 报导；\[法\] 新闻, 消息, 新闻报导
- 英文含义：New information of interest.
- 词形：news

**来源记录 1**（名词；New information of interest.）

From Middle English newes, newys (“new things”), equivalent to new (noun) + -s (plural suffix). Compare Saterland Frisian Näis (“news”), East Frisian näjs (“news”), West Frisian nijs (“news”), Dutch nieuws (“news”), German Low German Neeis (“new things; news”), though, unlike the English word, those cognates originated as genitives, not as plurals. Sometimes erroneously claimed to be an acronym of "North, East, West, South" or "Noteworthy Events, Weather, Sports".

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. news

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /njuːz/；美 /nuːz/

**中文释义**：n. 新闻, 消息, 报导；\[法\] 新闻, 消息, 新闻报导

**英文义项**：New information of interest. / Information about current events disseminated by the media.

**构词拆分**：new (noun) + -s (plural suffix)

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English newes, newys (“new things”), equivalent to new (noun) + -s (plural suffix). Compare Saterland Frisian Näis (“news”), East Frisian näjs (“news”), West Frisian nijs (“news”), Dutch nieuws (“news”), German Low German Neeis (“new things; news”), though, unlike the English word, those cognates originated as genitives, not as plurals. Sometimes erroneously claimed to be an acronym of "North, East, West, South" or "Noteworthy Events, Weather, Sports".

**例句**：

> The news is that a new leader will be elected in one month.

该用例对应义项：Information about current events disseminated by the media.

### 2. newspaper

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈnjuːsˌpeɪpə/；美 /ˈn(j)usˌpeɪpɚ/

**中文释义**：n. 报纸

**英文义项**：A publication, usually published daily or weekly, containing news and other articles. Traditionally a print publication typically printed on cheap, low-quality paper; today usually digital and often also available in print. / The news organization that produces such a publication.

**构词拆分**：news + paper

**词源与演化**：

From news + paper.

**例句**：

> The paper boy delivers her newspaper to her front porch every weekday.

该用例对应义项：A publication, usually published daily or weekly, containing news and other articles. Traditionally a print publication typically printed on cheap, low-quality paper; today usually digital and often also available in print.
