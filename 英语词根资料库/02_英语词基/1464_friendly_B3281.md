## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. friendly

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹɛnd.li/

**中文释义**：a. 友好的, 亲切的, 互助的；adv. 友善地, 温和地

**英文义项**：Generally warm, approachable and easy to relate with in character. / Inviting, characteristic of friendliness.

**构词拆分**：friend + -ly

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English frendly, freendly, frendely, frendlich, from Old English frēondlīċ, from Proto-Germanic \*frijōndlīkaz, equivalent to friend + -ly. Cognate with Saterland Frisian früntelk, fjuntelk (“friendly”), West Frisian freonlik (“friendly”), Dutch vriendelijk (“friendly”), German Low German fründelk, frünnelk (“friendly”), German freundlich (“friendly”). Doublet of friendlike.

**例句**：

> Our sandwiches are made with dolphin-friendly tuna.

该用例对应义项：Compatible with, or not damaging to (the compounded noun).

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. unfriendly

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ʌnˈfɹɛn(d)li/

**中文释义**：adv. 不友善地

**英文义项**：Not friendly; hostile; mean. / Unfavourable.

**构词拆分**：un- + friendly

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English unfrendly, unfrendli, unfrendely, from Old English \*unfrēondlīċ (suggested by derivative unfrēondlīċe (“in an unfriendly manner; unfriendly”, adverb)), equivalent to un- + friendly. Cognate with Saterland Frisian uunfrüntelk, uunfjuntelk (“unfriendly”), West Frisian ûnfreonlik (“unfriendly”), Dutch onvriendelijk (“unfriendly”), German Low German unfrünnelk (“unfriendly”), German unfreundlich (“unfriendly”), Faroese ófryntligur (“unfriendly”), Icelandic ófrýnilegur (“ugly; disturbing”).

**例句**：

> An unfriendly act of aggression.

该用例对应义项：not friendly

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. friendliness

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹɛndlinəs/

**中文释义**：n. 友谊, 亲切, 亲密

**英文义项**：The quality of being friendly.

**构词拆分**：friendly + -ness

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English freendlynesse, frendlynes, equivalent to friendly + -ness.

**例句**：

> I bought this phone because I was drawn to its user friendliness.
