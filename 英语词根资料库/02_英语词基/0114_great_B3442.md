### 2. greatness

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈɡɹeɪtnəs/

**中文释义**：n. 大, 巨大, 广大

**英文义项**：The state, condition, or quality of being great

**构词拆分**：great + -ness

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English gretnesse, gretnes, greetnesse, from Old English grēatnes. Equivalent to great + -ness.

**例句**：
