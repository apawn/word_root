## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. needed

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈniː.dɪd/；美 /ˈni.dɪd/

**中文释义**：a. 需要的

**英文义项**：Necessary; being required.

**构词拆分**：need + -ed

**词源与演化**：

From need + -ed. The adjective is derived from the verb.

**用法示例／搭配（非完整句）**：

> Just-in-time as-needed support for in-house training

该用例对应义项：Necessary; being required.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. unneeded

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ʌnˈniːdid/

**中文释义**：a. 不需要的

**英文义项**：Not needed.

**构词拆分**：un- + needed

**词源与演化**：

From un- + needed.

**例句**：

> Consider donating unneeded or non-perishable items to those in need.
