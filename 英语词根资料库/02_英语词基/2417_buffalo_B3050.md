## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. buffalo

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbʌf.əl.əʊ/；美 /ˈbʌf.ə.loʊ/

**中文释义**：n. 水牛；\[机\] 水牛

**英文义项**：An animal from the subtribe Bubalina, also known as true buffalos, such as the Cape buffalo, Syncerus caffer, or the water buffalo, Bubalus bubalis. / A related North American animal, the American bison, Bison bison.

**词源与演化**：

Borrowed from Portuguese or Spanish búfalo (“buffalo”), from Late Latin būfalus, from Latin būbalus, from Ancient Greek βούβαλος (boúbalos, “antelope, wild ox”). Doublet of bubale and buffle.

**例句**：

> For lions, a buffalo is much easier to kill than a giraffe.
