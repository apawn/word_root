## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. believer

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /bɪˈliːvə/；美 /bɪˈlivɚ/

**中文释义**：n. 信徒

**英文义项**：A person who believes; especially regarding religion.

**构词拆分**：believe + -er

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English bilevar, bileever, belever, equivalent to believe + -er.

**例句**：

> She's a believer in life after death.

该用例对应义项：A person who believes; especially regarding religion.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. unbeliever

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/.ʌnbiˈliːvə/

**中文释义**：n. 不相信者, 无宗教信仰者

**英文义项**：One who does not believe, particularly in a deity (used by believers to describe people who do not believe in the same deity or deities as themselves).

**构词拆分**：un- + believer

**词源与演化**：

From un- + believer, perhaps a corruption of earlier wanbeliever (“unbeliever”).

**例句**：

> Are you a total unbeliever or do you believe in Chance?
