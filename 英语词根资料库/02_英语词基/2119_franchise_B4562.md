## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 公民权, 特权, 特许经营权, 免赔额；vt. 给以特权, 给以...公民权
- 英文含义：The right to vote at a public election or referendum; see: suffrage.
- 词形：franchise

**多个词源／构词路径**：同一拼写可能有不同来源；以下按词性和含义分别列出。

**来源记录 1**（名词；The right to vote at a public election or referendum; see: suffrage.）

From Middle English franchise, fraunchise, from Old French franchise (“freedom”), a derivative of franc (“free”). More at frank.

**来源记录 2**（动词；To confer certain powers on; grant a franchise to; authorize.）

From Middle English franchisen, fraunchisen, from Old French franchir (stem franchiss-, “to set free”), from franc (“free”). More at frank.

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. franchise

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfɹænt͡ʃaɪ̯z/

**中文释义**：n. 公民权, 特权, 特许经营权, 免赔额；vt. 给以特权, 给以...公民权

**英文义项**：The right to vote at a public election or referendum; see: suffrage. / A right or privilege officially granted to a person, a group of people, or a company by a government.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English franchise, fraunchise, from Old French franchise (“freedom”), a derivative of franc (“free”). More at frank.

**例句**：

> The Whalers' home city of Hartford was one of many for the franchise.

该用例对应义项：The collection of organizations in the history of a sports team; the tradition of a sports team as an entity, extending beyond the contemporary organization.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. franchisee

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˌfrænt͡ʃaɪ̯ˈziː/

**中文释义**：n. 有代销权的人(或团体)；\[经\] 特许证持有人

**英文义项**：A holder of a franchise; a person who is granted a franchise.

**构词拆分**：From franchise + -ee.

**词源与演化**：

From franchise + -ee.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. franchisor

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/,fræntʃaiˈzɔː(r)/

**中文释义**：n. 授予特许者

**英文义项**：A company which, or a person who, grants franchises.

**构词拆分**：From franchise + -or.

**词源与演化**：

From franchise + -or.
