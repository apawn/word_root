# 2433 · journalism — n. 新闻业, 报章杂志；\[法\] 报刊, 新闻业, 新闻出版

[总目录](../00_总目录_按常用程度.md)

**类型**：英语自由词基　｜　**来源语言**：见词源记录

## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 新闻业, 报章杂志；\[法\] 报刊, 新闻业, 新闻出版
- 英文含义：The aggregating, writing, editing, and presenting of news or news articles for widespread distribution, typically in electronic publications, broadcast news media, or printed newspapers or periodicals, for the purpose of informing the audience, relying on a style of writing characteristic for this purpose, consisting of direct presentation of facts or events and (depending on type) either with or without analysis or interpretation.
- 词形：journalism

**来源记录 1**（名词；The aggregating, writing, editing, and presenting of news or news articles for widespread distribution, typically in electronic publications, broadcast news media, or printed newspapers or periodicals, for the purpose of informing the audience, relying on a style of writing characteristic for this purpose, consisting of direct presentation of facts or events and (depending on type) either with or without analysis or interpretation.）

From French journalisme (beginning of 19th century). By surface analysis, journal + -ism.

### 1. journalism

**中文释义**：n. 新闻业, 报章杂志；\[法\] 报刊, 新闻业, 新闻出版

**英文义项**：The aggregating, writing, editing, and presenting of news or news articles for widespread distribution, typically in electronic publications, broadcast news media, or printed newspapers or periodicals, for the purpose of informing the audience, relying on a style of writing characteristic for this purpose, consisting of direct presentation of facts or events and (depending on type) either with or without analysis or interpretation.

### 2. photojournalism

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˌfəʊ̯.təʊ̯ˈd͡ʒɜːn(ə)lɪzəm/；美 /ˌfoʊ̯.toʊ̯ˈd͡ʒɝn(ə)lɪzəm/

**中文释义**：n. 摄影新闻工作, 摄影报道

**英文义项**：A form of journalism in which a story is told primarily through photographs and other images.

**构词拆分**：photo- + journalism

**词源与演化**：

From photo- + journalism.
