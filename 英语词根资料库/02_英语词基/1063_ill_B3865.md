- 词形：ill

**来源记录 1**（形容词；Indicative of unkind or malevolent intentions; harsh, cruel.）

From Middle English ille (“evil; wicked”), from Old Norse íllr (adjective), ílla (adverb), ílt (noun), from Proto-Germanic \*ilhilaz, from Proto-Indo-European \*h₁elḱ- (whence Latin ulcus (“sore”), Ancient Greek ἕλκος (hélkos, “wound, ulcer”), Sanskrit अर्शस् (árśas, “hemorrhoids”)). Cognates Cognate with Scots and Yola ill, Danish ilde (“bad”), Faroese, Icelandic illur (“bad, ill, wicked”), Norwegian Bokmål, Norwegian Nynorsk ille (“bad”), Swedish illa (“badly; poorly”).

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. ill

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ɪl/

**中文释义**：n. 疾病, 坏事, 罪恶, 灾难；a. 生病的, 邪恶的, 不吉利的, 敌意的, 不良的, 不顺利的；adv. 有害地, 不幸地, 几乎不

**英文义项**：Indicative of unkind or malevolent intentions; harsh, cruel. / Unpropitious, unkind, faulty, not up to reasonable standard.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English ille (“evil; wicked”), from Old Norse íllr (adjective), ílla (adverb), ílt (noun), from Proto-Germanic \*ilhilaz, from Proto-Indo-European \*h₁elḱ- (whence Latin ulcus (“sore”), Ancient Greek ἕλκος (hélkos, “wound, ulcer”), Sanskrit अर्शस् (árśas, “hemorrhoids”)). Cognates Cognate with Scots and Yola ill, Danish ilde (“bad”), Faroese, Icelandic illur (“bad, ill, wicked”), Norwegian Bokmål, Norwegian Nynorsk ille (“bad”), Swedish illa (“badly; poorly”).

**例句**：

> I've been ill with the flu for the past few days.

该用例对应义项：Unwell in terms of health or physical condition; sick.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. illness

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈɪl.nəs/

**中文释义**：n. 疾病, 恶意；\[医\] 病

**英文义项**：An instance (episode) of a disease or poor health. / A state of bad health or disease.

**构词拆分**：ill + -ness

**词源与演化**：

From ill + -ness.

**例句**：

> Many working days this year have been lost through illness.

该用例对应义项：A state of bad health or disease.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. illy

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈili/

**中文释义**：adv. 不完美地；困难的；不善地, 坏地

**英文义项**：cool, fresh

**构词拆分**：ill + -ly

**词源与演化**：

From ill + -ly.
