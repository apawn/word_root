## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 赞美, 称赞, 崇拜；vt. 称赞, 赞美；vi. 赞扬, 表扬
- 英文含义：Commendation; favourable representation in words.
- 词形：praise

**多个词源／构词路径**：同一拼写可能有不同来源；以下按词性和含义分别列出。

**来源记录 1**（名词；Commendation; favourable representation in words.）

From Middle English praise, preyse, from the verb (see below). Doublet of prize. Displaced native Middle English lof from Old English lof (“praise”) and Middle English loenge, loange from Old French löenge, löange (“praise”).

**来源记录 2**（动词；To give praise to; to commend, glorify, or worship.）

From Middle English praisen, preisen, from Old French proisier, preisier (“to value, prize”), from Late Latin pretiō (“to value, prize”) from pretium (“price, worth, reward”). Displaced native Middle English herien from Old English herian (“to praise”).

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. praise

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /pɹeɪz/

**中文释义**：n. 赞美, 称赞, 崇拜；vt. 称赞, 赞美；vi. 赞扬, 表扬

**英文义项**：Commendation; favourable representation in words. / Worship, glorification, adoration.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English praise, preyse, from the verb (see below). Doublet of prize. Displaced native Middle English lof from Old English lof (“praise”) and Middle English loenge, loange from Old French löenge, löange (“praise”).

**例句**：

> She gave them some faint praise for their assignments, despite not being totally convinced by the quality.

该用例对应义项：Commendation; favourable representation in words.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. praiseworthy

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈpɹeɪz.wɜː.ði/；美 /ˈpɹeɪz.wɝ.ði/

**中文释义**：a. 值得称赞的, 令人钦佩的, 可钦佩的

**英文义项**：Meriting praise; worthy of high praise

**构词拆分**：From praise + -worthy.

**词源与演化**：

From praise + -worthy.

**例句**：

> A significant and praiseworthy increase in computer intelligence.

该用例对应义项：worthy of high praise
