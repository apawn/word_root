## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. celebrity

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /sɪˈlɛb.ɹɪ.ti/；美 /səˈlɛb.ɹɪ.ɾi/

**中文释义**：n. 名声, 名人

**英文义项**：Fame, renown; the state of being famous or talked-about. / A person who has a high degree of recognition by the general population for his or her success or accomplishments; a famous person.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English celebritē, from Old French celebrite (compare French célébrité), from Latin celēbritās.

**例句**：

> Many of those that envy celebrities aspire to celebrity themselves.

该用例对应义项：Fame, renown; the state of being famous or talked-about.
