## 核心含义与来源

- 核心含义：n. 广播, 传播；a. 广播的；v. 广播
- 英文含义：Cast or scattered widely in all directions; cast abroad.
- 词形：broadcast

**来源记录 1**（形容词；Cast or scattered widely in all directions; cast abroad.）

From broad + cast. First attested in the mid 18th century, in the agricultural use of spreading seeds.

## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. broadcast

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbɹɔːd(ˌ)kɑːst/；/ˈbɹɔd(ˌ)kæst/

**中文释义**：n. 广播, 传播；a. 广播的；v. 广播

**英文义项**：Cast or scattered widely in all directions; cast abroad. / Communicated, signalled, or transmitted to many people, through radio waves or electronic means.

**构词拆分**：broad + cast

**词源与演化**：

From broad + cast. First attested in the mid 18th century, in the agricultural use of spreading seeds.

**例句**：

> The seed was broadcast, not drilled.

该用例对应义项：Cast or scattered widely in all directions; cast abroad.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. broadcaster

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈbɹɔːdˌkɑːstə/；美 /ˈbɹɔdˌkæstɚ/

**中文释义**：n. 播送者, 广播装置, 播种机

**英文义项**：A machine used to broadcast or spread seeds, fertilizer, etc. / A piece of equipment used to transmit audio and/or video content, or messages, to be received by radios or televisions, over the internet, etc.

**构词拆分**：broadcast (“to transmit a message or signal through radio waves or electronic means”) + -er (suffix forming agent nouns)

**词源与演化**：

From broadcast (“to transmit a message or signal through radio waves or electronic means”) + -er (suffix forming agent nouns).

**例句**：

> I saw it in a documentary produced by NHK, Japan's public broadcaster.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. rebroadcast

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/.riːˈbrɒːdkɑːst/

**中文释义**：v. 转播, 重播；n. 转播, 重播

**英文义项**：Retransmission; a repeated or relayed broadcast.

**构词拆分**：re- + broadcast

**词源与演化**：

From re- + broadcast.
