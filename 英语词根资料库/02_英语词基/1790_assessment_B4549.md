### 1. assessment

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/əˈsɛsmənt/

**中文释义**：n. 评估, 估定, 评定的款额；\[化\] 评估

**英文义项**：The act of assessing or an amount (of tax, levy or duty etc) assessed. / An appraisal or evaluation.

**构词拆分**：assess + -ment

**词源与演化**：

From assess + -ment.

**例句**：
