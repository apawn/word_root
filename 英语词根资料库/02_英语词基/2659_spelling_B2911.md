## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. spelling

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈspɛl.ɪŋ/

**中文释义**：n. 拼, 拼字, 拼法；\[计\] 拼写检查

**英文义项**：The act, practice, ability, or subject of forming words with letters, or of reading the letters of words; orthography. / The manner of spelling of words; correct spelling.

**构词拆分**：spell (verb) + -ing

**词源与演化**：

1400s, from spell (verb) + -ing.

**例句**：

> Many words are pronounced according to the spelling, but some are not.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. misspelling

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/.misˈspeliŋ/

**中文释义**：n. 拼错

**英文义项**：Incorrect spelling. / A misspelled word.

**构词拆分**：mis- + spelling

**词源与演化**：

From mis- + spelling.

**例句**：

> Accomodation is a common misspelling of accommodation.

该用例对应义项：A misspelled word.
