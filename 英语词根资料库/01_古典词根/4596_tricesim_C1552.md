## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. trigesimal

**词性**：形容词

**英文义项**：Thirtieth. / Containing thirty of something.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin trigesimus (“thirtieth”) + -al (“forming adjectives”).

**例句**：

> Each month is a roughly trigesimal period.

该用例对应义项：Containing thirty of something.
