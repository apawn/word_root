## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. et cetera

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˌɛt ˈsɛt(ə)ɹə/

**中文释义**：以及其他等等, ...等等

**英文义项**：Something in addition, which can easily be understood.

**词源与演化**：

From Middle English et cetera, from Latin et cētera (“and the other things; and the rest of the things”).
