### 1. strigose

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈstɹaɪɡəʊs/

**中文释义**：a. 有细密槽纹的

**英文义项**：Having fine grooves, ridges, or streaks. / Having stiff hairs, pressed together.

**词源与演化**：
