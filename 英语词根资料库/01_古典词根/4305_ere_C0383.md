## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. trireme

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈtɹaɪ.ɹiːm/

**中文释义**：n. 三列桨船

**英文义项**：A galley with three banks of oars, one above the other, used mainly as a warship.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin trirēmis, from tri- + rēmus (“oar”).

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. trierarch

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈtrai,rɑːk/

**中文释义**：(古希腊)三层桨(座)战船的司令官

**英文义项**：The captain of a trireme.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin trierarchus.
