## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. pelite

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈpiːlait/

**中文释义**：n. \[地\]泥质岩

**英文义项**：A sedimentary rock containing very fine particles.

**词源与演化**：

From German Pelit, corresponding to Ancient Greek πηλός (pēlós, “mud, clay”) + -ite.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. pelitic

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/piˈlitik/

**中文释义**：\[地质\]泥质岩的

**英文义项**：Relating to, or containing, pelite.

**构词拆分**：pelite + -ic

**词源与演化**：

From pelite + -ic.
