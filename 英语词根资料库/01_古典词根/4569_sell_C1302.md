### 1. sella turcica

**词性**：名词

**中文释义**：\[医\] 蝶鞍

**英文义项**：A saddle-shaped depression in the sphenoid bone of the skull in humans and other hominids.

**词源与演化**：

Latin: sella + turcica = literally “Turkish chair”.
