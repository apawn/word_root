## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. ombrogenous

**词性**：形容词

**英文义项**：Dependant on rain.

**构词拆分**：ombro- + -genous

**词源与演化**：

ombro- + -genous

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. ombrology

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ɒmˈbrɒlədʒi/

**中文释义**：n. 测雨学

**英文义项**：The study of rainfall.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. ombrometer

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ɔmˈbrɔmitə/

**中文释义**：n. 雨量计, 雨计

**英文义项**：A rain gauge

**构词拆分**：ombro- + -meter

**词源与演化**：

From ombro- + -meter.

<a id="w-4"></a>

### 4. ombrophilous

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈɒmbəɒfələs/

**中文释义**：\[医\]喜雨的

**英文义项**：Capable of thriving in areas of heavy rainfall

**构词拆分**：ombro- + -philous

**词源与演化**：

From ombro- + -philous.

<a id="w-5"></a>

### 5. ombrophobe

**词性**：形容词

**中文释义**：避雨植物

**英文义项**：Synonym of ombrophobic.

<a id="w-6"></a>

### 6. ombrotrophic

**词性**：形容词

**英文义项**：Pertaining to ombrotrophy.

**构词拆分**：ombro- + -trophic

**词源与演化**：

From ombro- + -trophic.
