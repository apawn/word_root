### 1. mysophilia

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˌmaɪsəʊ̆ˈfɪlɪə/

**中文释义**：n. \[医\]恋秽癖,嗜污癖

**英文义项**：A sexual dependency on something soiled or filthy, usually a kind of undergarment after use.

**构词拆分**：myso- + -philia

**词源与演化**：

From myso- + -philia.
