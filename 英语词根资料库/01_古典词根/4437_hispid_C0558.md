### 1. hispidity

**词性**：名词

**英文义项**：The quality of being hispid.

**构词拆分**：hispid + -ity

**词源与演化**：

From hispid + -ity.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. hispidulous

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/hisˈpidjuləs/

**中文释义**：a. \[生\]具短硬毛的

**英文义项**：Minutely hispid; having tiny hairs.

**词源与演化**：

Diminutive of hispid. From Latin hispidul(us) + -ous. By surface analysis, hispid + -ule + -ous.
