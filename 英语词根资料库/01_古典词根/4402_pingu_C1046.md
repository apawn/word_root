## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. pinguicula

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/pɪŋˈɡaɪkjʊlə/

**中文释义**：\[医\]结膜黄斑, 睑裂黄斑

**英文义项**：Any of the genus Pinguicula of butterworts.

**词源与演化**：

From the genus name translingual Pinguicula.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. pinguitude

**词性**：名词

**英文义项**：fatness; obesity

**词源与演化**：

From Latin pinguitudo, from pinguis (“fat”).
