## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. geitonogamy

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/,^aitəˈnɔ^əmi/

**中文释义**：n. \[植\]同株授粉

**英文义项**：Transfer of pollen grains from the anther of a flower to the stigma of another flower of the same plant.

**词源与演化**：

From Ancient Greek γείτων (geítōn, “neighbour”, geitono-) + -gamy (“marriage”).
