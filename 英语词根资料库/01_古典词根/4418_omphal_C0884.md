## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. omphalectomy

**词性**：名词

**中文释义**：\[医\] 脐切除术

**英文义项**：The excision of the navel.

**构词拆分**：omphal- + -ectomy

**词源与演化**：

From omphal- + -ectomy.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. omphalic

**词性**：形容词

**中文释义**：\[医\] 脐的

**英文义项**：Of or pertaining to the umbilicus, or navel.

<a id="w-3"></a>

### 3. omphalopagus

**词性**：名词

**中文释义**：\[医\] 脐部联胎

**英文义项**：Joined at the umbilicus.

**构词拆分**：omphalo- + -pagus

**词源与演化**：

From omphalo- + -pagus.

<a id="w-4"></a>

### 4. omphalophobia

**词性**：名词

**英文义项**：Fear of navels.

**构词拆分**：omphalo- + -phobia

**词源与演化**：

From omphalo- + -phobia.
