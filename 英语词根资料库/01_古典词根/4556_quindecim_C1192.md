## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. quindecimal

**词性**：形容词

**英文义项**：Based on the number fifteen, base 15.

**构词拆分**：quindecim + -al

**词源与演化**：

From Latin quindecimalis, from quindecim (“15”) + -ālis (“-al”), from quinque (“5”) + decem (“10”). Equivalent to quindecim + -al.
