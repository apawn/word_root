### 1. quindenary

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/ˈkwind(ə)nəri/

**中文释义**：十五进制的

**英文义项**：Containing fifteen things, or to the base of fifteen.

**构词拆分**：quindene + -ary

**词源与演化**：

From Latin quindēnārius (“containing fifteen”). Equivalent to quindene + -ary.
