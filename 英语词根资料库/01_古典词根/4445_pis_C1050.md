<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. pisoid

**词性**：名词

**英文义项**：A concretionary grain, typically of calcium carbonate, between a microid and a macroid in size.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin pisum + -oid.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. pisolite

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈpaisəlait/

**中文释义**：n. 豆石, 豆岩

**英文义项**：A sedimentary rock formed from pisoids

**词源与演化**：

From Latin pisum + -lite.
