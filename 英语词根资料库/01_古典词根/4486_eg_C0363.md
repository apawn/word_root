### 1. egophony

**词性**：名词

**中文释义**：\[医\] 羊音

**英文义项**：A bleating sound sometimes observed in patients with various lung conditions.

**构词拆分**：ego- (“goat”) + -phony

**词源与演化**：

From ego- (“goat”) + -phony.
