### 1. palustral

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/pəˈlʌstɹəl/

**中文释义**：a. 沼泽的, (鸟等)沼栖的, 沼生的

**英文义项**：Pertaining to or located in marshes; marshy. / That requires a marshy habitat.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin palustris, from palūs (“swamp”), -al.
