## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. salicin

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈsælisin/

**中文释义**：n. 水杨苷；\[化\] 水杨苷

**英文义项**：A glucoside derivative of salicylic acid; the active principle of willow bark, once used medicinally.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin salix, salicis (“a willow”) + -in.
