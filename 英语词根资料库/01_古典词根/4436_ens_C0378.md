### 1. ensiferous

**词性**：形容词

**英文义项**：sword-bearing.

**构词拆分**：ensifer + -ous

**词源与演化**：

From ensifer + -ous.

<a id="w-2"></a>

### 2. ensiform

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：美 /ˈɛnsəˌfɔɹm/

**中文释义**：a. 剑形的；\[医\] 剑形的

**英文义项**：shaped like a sword blade

**词源与演化**：

From Latin ensis (“sword”) + -form.

**例句**：

> The iris has an ensiform leaf.

该用例对应义项：shaped like a sword blade
