### 1. leukopenia

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/.luːkəuˈpiːniə/

**中文释义**：n. 白血球减少症；\[医\] 白细胞减少

**英文义项**：An abnormally low count of leukocytes, or white blood cells, in the blood.

**构词拆分**：leuko- + -penia

**词源与演化**：

From leuko- + -penia.
