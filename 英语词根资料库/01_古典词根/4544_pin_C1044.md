### 1. pineal gland

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：英 /ˈpaɪ.niːəl ˌɡlænd/

**中文释义**：\[化\] 松果腺

**英文义项**：A small, pinecone-shaped endocrine gland found near the centre of the brain that produces melatonin.

**构词拆分**：pineal + gland

**词源与演化**：

From pineal + gland, from its pine-cone shape.
