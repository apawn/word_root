## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. scolex

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈskəuleks/

**中文释义**：n. 绦虫, 头节；\[医\] 头节

**英文义项**：The structure at the front end of a tapeworm which, in the adult, has suckers and hooks by which it attaches itself to a host.

**词源与演化**：

Borrowed from Ancient Greek σκώληξ (skṓlēx, “worm”).
