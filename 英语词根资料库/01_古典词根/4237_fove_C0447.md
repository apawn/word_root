### 1. fovea

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈfəʊ.vi.ə/

**中文释义**：n. 窝, 凹；\[医\] 凹

**英文义项**：A slight depression or pit in a bone or organ. / The retinal fovea, or fovea centralis, responsible for sharp central vision.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin fovea (“ditch, pit”). Doublet of foiba.
