### 1. tetartohedral

**词性**：形容词　｜　**音标**：/tiˈtɑːtəuˈhiːdrəl/

**中文释义**：a. 具有四分之一结晶体对称面的

**英文义项**：isometric and tetragonal, but having one fourth of the number of planes required for complete symmetry

**词源与演化**：

From Ancient Greek τέταρτος (tétartos) + -hedral.
