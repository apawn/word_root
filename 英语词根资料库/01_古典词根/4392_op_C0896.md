## 逐词详解

<a id="w-1"></a>

### 1. metope

**词性**：名词　｜　**音标**：/ˈmɛtəʊp/

**中文释义**：n. 三槽板间平面

**英文义项**：The architectural element between two triglyphs in a Doric frieze.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin metopa and its source, Koine Greek μετόπη (metópē), from μετά (metá, “between”) + ὀπή (opḗ, “hole”).
