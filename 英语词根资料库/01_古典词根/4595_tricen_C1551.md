### 1. tricenary

**词性**：形容词

**英文义项**：Of or related to the number thirty. / Lasting thirty days.

**词源与演化**：

From Latin trīcēnārius, from trīcēnus (“thirty each”) + -ārius (“-ary: forming adjectives and related nouns”).
